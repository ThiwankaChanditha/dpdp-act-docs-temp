## What the accelerator covers

### Consent and privacy operations

Create and manage purposes, elements, consent decisions, withdrawals, and
consent history through the Consent Portal and Identity Server APIs.

### Grievance handling

Give Data Principals a place to submit and track complaints while enabling
officers, DPOs, and administrators to assign, investigate, communicate, and
resolve cases.

### Event notifications

Publish consent and lifecycle events to subscribed applications through
webhooks or polling, with delivery history and completion handling for
downstream processors.

### Tenant-aware administration

Use role-based access and tenant-scoped records to separate Data Principal
self-service from administrator and developer operations.
